# Mortgage calculator V2: Independent review

## How the review was done

- **Reviewer:** a subagent with a fresh context, given `docs/v2/01-functional.md`, `02-architecture.md`, `03-test-scenarios.md` and, as the V1 reference, `docs/v1/01-functional.md` and `docs/v1/03-test-scenarios.md`. It could not modify files and had to recompute every reference value in its own script.
- **Round 1** reviewed the first drafts of 01 to 03 (extra payment, save, saved list, no schedule).
- **After round 1** the scope grew: the owner asked for a repayment schedule and a balance chart (FR11, FR12), and the author found a flaw in the first design (finding A1 below). 01 to 03 were rewritten once with all decisions. **Round 2** reviews that second version (see the bottom of this file).
- **Limit of the "clean context":** a subagent is clean of the author's reasoning, not of the environment.

## Round 2: reviewer findings and decisions

Reviewer: a second fresh-context subagent, the same prompt (with the schedule and chart in scope) and 20,000 random cases for the claims "the extra schedule is never longer and never costs more" and "the standard schedule always ends at 0 within the term". Result: **no blockers**, 9 major, 8 minor. **All reference values matched again**, and both claims held on 30,000 random inputs plus a 12,992-case grid of tiny loans and extras. The findings are again about unstated rules.

**Open risk noted by the reviewer and accepted:** a rate above 0 with an early payoff exists (17 of 200,000 random cases, for example 1,010.15 €, 0.18 %, 468 months ends at month 467). The schedule rule handles it, and the random identity test `schedule-random-inputs-keep-the-identities` covers it.

**Number of rounds:** 2. No third round: the findings of round 2 were rules and tests, and no reference value changed.
