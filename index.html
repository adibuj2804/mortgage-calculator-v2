Mortgage jargon, explained

What is principal?

The amount you actually borrowed: the property price minus your down payment.

What is interest?

The bank's fee for lending you money. Early on most of each payment is interest; over time more goes to principal.

What is an annuity payment?

A payment that stays the same every month. It is calculated so the loan reaches exactly zero on the last payment.

What is a down payment?

Your own money put in upfront. A bigger down payment means a smaller loan and less interest overall.

What is overpayment?

Total interest as a percentage of the loan. 67% means you pay back the loan plus another 67% of it in interest.

Quick tips
Compare termsA longer term lowers the monthly payment but costs much more interest.
